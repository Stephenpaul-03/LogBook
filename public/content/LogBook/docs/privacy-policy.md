# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. It is LogBook's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect while operating our application.

## 1. Information We Collect

LogBook is a client-side application served from static content.
- No login, registration, contact form, payment flow, or user-content submission flow is implemented in this repository.
- The app stores the selected theme and custom context-menu preference locally in your browser's `localStorage`; it does not store recently visited pages.
- The app itself does not intentionally transmit personal information, but normal page requests still reach the static host and may be recorded in infrastructure logs as described below.

## 2. Third-Party Services

LogBook does not integrate with any tracking services, advertisements, or application database.

## 3. Hosting and request logs

The static host, CDN, reverse proxy, or security services used to deploy LogBook may
process technical request data such as IP addresses, timestamps, user-agent strings,
and requested URLs. The operator must complete this section with the actual providers,
purposes, retention periods, and contact details before publishing this policy.
